Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters at SkinGlam

1. What We Collect

When you visit SkinGlam, we may collect personal data including your name, email, phone number, skin type, product preferences, and order history. We may also track usage data through cookies and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to provide product recommendations, improve your experience, process orders, send order updates, and notify you of special offers or promotions. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. How We Protect Your Data

Your data is securely stored and protected with encryption. We do not sell your information to third parties. Only authorized SkinGlam staff can access your information for customer service and order processing.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to view, edit, or delete your personal data at any time. Simply email us at support@skinglam.com. You may also request to stop receiving marketing emails.
