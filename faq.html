Frequently Asked Questions

Your SkinGlam Questions Answered

How can I get personalized skin care recommendations?
You can use our skin analysis tool to get personalized product recommendations tailored to your skin type and concerns.
What is SkinGlam’s return policy?
We accept returns within 14 days of purchase as long as the products are unopened and in their original packaging.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes! We ship internationally. Shipping fees and delivery times vary depending on the destination.
How do I contact customer support?
You can contact us via the Contact page, email us at support@skinglam.com, or reach out through our social media channels.
Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?
Absolutely! All SkinGlam products are cruelty-free and formulated without any animal-derived ingredients.
Can I track my order after purchase?
Yes, once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email to monitor your delivery status.
