Understanding Sales Reports

Step 1: Access the Admin Dashboard

Log in with your admin credentials and open the dashboard.

Step 2: Navigate to “Sales Reports”

From the menu, click on "Reports" or "Sales" to access reporting tools.

Step 3: Select Date Range

  • Choose the time period you want to analyze (e.g., last week, this month).
  • Apply the filter to generate the report for that period.

Step 4: Review Key Metrics

  • Total Sales: Total revenue during the selected period.
  • Orders: Number of completed transactions.
  • Average Order Value: Total sales divided by number of orders.
  • Top Products: List of best-selling items.

Step 5: Export Reports

Use the export button to download the report as PDF or Excel for offline use or meetings.

Step 6: Use Insights for Decision Making

Use the sales data to identify trends, improve products, and adjust pricing or marketing strategy.
