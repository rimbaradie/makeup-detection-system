How to Add a New Product

Step 1: Login to the Admin Dashboard

Use your admin credentials to log into the system and access the dashboard.

Step 2: Navigate to the Product Management Section

From the sidebar or top menu, click on "Products" and then select "Add New Product".

Step 3: Fill Out the Product Form

  • Product Name: Enter the name of the product.
  • Price: Set the product price.
  • Description: Write a short description.
  • Category: Choose a category from the dropdown.
  • Image: Upload a product image (JPG or PNG).

Step 4: Save the Product

After completing the form, click on the "Save" or "Add Product" button. A confirmation message will appear once it's successfully saved.

Step 5: View the Product in the Product List

Go back to the product list to make sure your new product has been added and appears correctly.
